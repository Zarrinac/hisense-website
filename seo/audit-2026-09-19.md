All 108 page responses had private/no-store caching. Canonical/hreflang values agreed with the existing live sitemap, with no duplicate titles. Of 20 distinct live OG URLs, 19 were verified 200 after transient-timeout retries; the remaining RS-370 image was the fixed extension error. Both www and apex attempts for the sibling zarrinac homepage/product routes timed out. Prior cross-domain findings remain documented, but sibling redirects and Google's chosen canonical were **not newly verified**. No authenticated Search Console data was available in this run.

Decorative/product clips are not automatically eligible video watch pages. [Google video guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/video).

- No arbitrary description-padding gate was added. Google does not specify a fixed meta-description length. [Google snippet guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet).
- A DB outage during a future build can legitimately select bundled fallback data; hourly ISR refreshes the catalog. Admin product writes are not given new immediate invalidation hooks in this task, so catalog/sitemap changes can take the normal revalidation interval to appear.
